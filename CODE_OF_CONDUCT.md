# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in Studio Recorder welcoming and harassment-free for everyone. Be respectful, accept constructive feedback, focus on what is best for the community, and show empathy toward other contributors.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, trolling, personal attacks, publishing private information, or other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

Project maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior they consider inappropriate. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers will review reports promptly and protect reporter confidentiality as far as possible.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
